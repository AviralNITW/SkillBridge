1. Executive Summary

SkillBridge is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform designed to connect students, educational institutions, mentors, vocational professionals, community organizations, and companies through a unified ecosystem focused on skill development, experiential learning, internships, apprenticeships, and employability enhancement.

The platform addresses the growing gap between academic education and practical skill acquisition by providing structured opportunities for students to participate in real-world learning experiences while enabling schools and organizations to monitor, evaluate, and manage student development activities efficiently.

The system aims to become a centralized hub where students can build professional portfolios, gain verified certifications, participate in internships, and develop industry-relevant skills.



2. Business Problem

Current educational systems face multiple operational challenges.

Student Challenges

Lack of access to verified internships.

Limited practical exposure.

Absence of digital portfolios.

Poor visibility of achievements.

Difficulty demonstrating employability skills.

School Challenges

No centralized internship management system.

Manual tracking of student activities.

Difficulty generating skill development reports.

Limited visibility into student progress.

Company Challenges

Difficulty identifying early talent.

High recruitment costs.

Lack of structured internship management.

Mentor Challenges

No platform for mentoring students.

Limited reach to interested learners.

Lack of evaluation and certification mechanisms.
