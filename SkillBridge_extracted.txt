1. Module Overview
The Identity & Access Management Module is responsible for authentication, authorization, user identity verification, account lifecycle management, session management, and security enforcement across the SkillBridge platform.
This module acts as the foundation of the entire platform because every protected operation depends on authenticated and authorized users.
The module supports multiple user roles:
Student
School Administrator
Company
Mentor
Platform Admin

2. Business Objectives
BO-IAM-001
Provide secure registration for all user types.
BO-IAM-002
Ensure only verified users can access protected resources.
BO-IAM-003
Enforce role-based access control.
BO-IAM-004
Provide secure login and session management.
BO-IAM-005
Support password recovery and account security.
BO-IAM-006
Maintain audit trail for security-sensitive actions.

Tab 15
The Event Catalog is the backbone of a modern system.
Without it:
Application Accepted
  ↓
Call Notification Service
  ↓
Call Analytics Service
  ↓
Call Audit Service
  ↓
Call Certificate Service
Everything becomes tightly coupled.
With Event Catalog:
Application Accepted
       ↓
APPLICATION_ACCEPTED Event
       ↓
Notification Service
Analytics Service
Audit Service
Workspace Service
Recommendation Service
Much cleaner.
